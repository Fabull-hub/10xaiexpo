Section 07

How to use this with Gemini.

Two techniques that eliminate color confusion:

Technique 1 — Screenshot method: Take a screenshot of Section 02 (the palette above) and drop it into Gemini alongside your prompt. Say: "Match these exact colors — especially the electric lime #B4FF3A."

Technique 2 — Reference method: Copy the DESIGN-SYSTEM.md file's "5 Quick Reference Card" section into your prompt as brand context, before your actual image request.

Both work. Screenshot method usually works better for color accuracy. Reference method usually works better for style/mood consistency.
